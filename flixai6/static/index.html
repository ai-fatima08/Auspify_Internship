              Content success analytics.From raw data to decisions.

              An end-to-end pipeline that validates the data, engineers features, trains and compares models, explains them, and writes the findings as business insights.

              Automated insights

              Written by the engine from the data and the model, ranked by business impact. Confidence reflects how many series the finding rests on.

              Where success happens

              The dot is the renewal rate and the line is its 95% range. The white marker is the average for all series. A long line means the rate is uncertain.

              Describe a series and the production model estimates its chance of a second season, then shows which single changes would raise or lower it. Genres with fewer than 40 series are not offered because the model cannot learn them reliably.
